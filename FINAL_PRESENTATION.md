Slide 3: What the Product Does

The problem: blockchain is often explained from the technology side first. That creates a gap between ecosystems and people with real business problems.

Many non-technical users do not know whether blockchain is relevant to their case. They also may not know when a simpler tool, a better process, or a clearer commercial path would be more appropriate.

CIL Market Bridge helps reduce that gap.

Who it is for:

small and medium businesses;
entrepreneurs;
organizations exploring trust, transparency, traceability, payments, certificates, disputes, or coordination problems;
non-technical users who need a clearer first step before investing in a platform, vendor, pilot, or technology.

What users can do:

enter the application from the landing page;
describe their business case;
select relevant concern areas;
receive a Free Case Map;
see the main pattern detected in their case;
edit the case without losing their information;
start a new case from zero;
request a deeper Beta Opportunity Path after payment.

The value: CIL Market Bridge helps users understand the problem before jumping into technology.

It does not automatically recommend blockchain. It identifies signals, caution areas, possible alternatives, and a practical next step.

Where payment happens: the paid Beta Opportunity Path is currently processed through Gumroad as an external checkout. After payment, the user receives access to the Tally request form for the deeper human reviewed case exploration.

Flow:

Landing page / CIL Market Bridge app

That review may include:

possible blockchain relevance;
caution signals;
simpler alternatives when appropriate;
a practical exploration path;
and a short human-reviewed note from CIL Market Bridge.

Slide 5: How a User Buys the Product

The Free Case Map is free.

The paid product is the Beta Opportunity Path, priced at USD 5.

The buying flow is:

The user completes the free case map.
The user chooses to unlock the Beta Opportunity Path.
The user pays through Gumroad.
Gumroad confirms the purchase.
The user receives the Tally request form.
CIL Market Bridge prepares a deeper human-reviewed case exploration.

The product is not a subscription.

It is a paid beta review per case.

CIL Market Bridge does not provide financial, legal, or investment advice.
The review is an exploratory business analysis to help the
user better understand whether blockchain may be relevant, whether a
simpler alternative may be enough, and what practical next step could be considered.

Short spoken script

Hi, I’m Juanita Jaramillo Rivillas, and I built CIL Market Bridge as a solo builder for Piece of Pie.

CIL Market Bridge is a guided beta tool that helps non-technical users explore whether a real business problem may have blockchain relevance.

The idea started from a simple observation: blockchain ecosystems often explain solutions from the technical side first. But businesses usually start somewhere else. They start with a problem: payments, trust, traceability, disputes, certificates, transparency, or coordination.

CIL Market Bridge starts there.

The user describes a case, selects the areas that feel relevant, and receives a Free Case Map. The result identifies a main pattern, explains what the selected areas may suggest, summarizes the business context, and gives a suggested next step.

The product does not automatically say “you need blockchain.” That is important. Sometimes the right answer may be blockchain. Sometimes it may be a simpler digital tool, a better process, a commercial validation step, or a coordination model before any technology is selected.

During the hackathon, the application evolved from a simple exploratory flow into a more complete beta experience. I improved the result logic, added combinations between concern areas, allowed users to edit their case or start over, updated the landing page, and connected the free experience with a paid Beta Opportunity Path.

The paid flow is now simple: after receiving the Free Case Map, the user can unlock the Beta Opportunity Path for USD 5 through Gumroad. After payment, the user receives a Tally form to submit the case for a deeper human-reviewed exploration.

That review includes possible blockchain relevance, caution signals, simpler alternatives when appropriate, and a practical next step.

This matters because the goal is not to push blockchain into every problem. The goal is to help people understand whether blockchain deserves to be explored at all.

For the demo, I will show the complete flow: from entering the app, describing a business case, receiving the Free Case Map, opening the paid Beta Opportunity Path, completing checkout, and reaching the request form.

CIL Market Bridge is still an early beta, but it now has a working user journey: problem exploration, free result, paid review, and real user testing.

Thank you.
