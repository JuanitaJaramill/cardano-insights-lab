## Week 2 Progress

This week focused on listening, validating, and refining the direction of CIL Market Bridge.

### What we learned

After gathering feedback from a small focus group, two important insights became clear:

- A traditional diagnostic form feels too rigid for real business problems.
- Many cases involve more than one dimension at the same time — such as payments, traceability, certificates, trust, or transparency — so a single-choice structure does not reflect how businesses actually think about their challenges.

### What we changed

Based on that feedback, we improved the CIL Market Bridge landing page.

Instead of pushing visitors directly into a form, the page now helps businesses reflect on whether they may be facing a problem worth exploring through a set of simple, business-oriented prompts around:

- payments,
- traceability,
- certificates and verification,
- trust between actors,
- transparency,
- disputes and authenticity.

The call to action was also adjusted to create a softer and more natural entry point:

**“Tell us about your case.”**

### Product direction refined

This week also helped clarify the role of each part of the project:

- **The landing page** should attract, orient, and guide visitors toward the product.
- **The app** will become the core experience, helping users turn an unclear business concern into a clearer evaluation path.
- **The future paid beta version** will offer a more complete opportunity path, including a short human-reviewed note from CIL.

The project is now moving toward an app-first development phase for Week 3.
